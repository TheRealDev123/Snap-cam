📖 Help — How To Use LumenCam (Docs)

📷 How to take a Photo

1. Make sure you are on PHOTO tab.
2. Tap the big white circle.
3. It flashes (if Flash is ON), downloads instantly, and appears in Gallery.

🎥 How to take a Video

1. Tap VIDEO tab.
2. Tap the red circle to start. Tap again to stop.
3. Video auto-saves + downloads to Gallery.

🌌 How to take a Space Video

1. Tap SPACE VIDEO.
2. App turns Light Mode, shows a reminder to turn your screen brightness to 100%.
3. Flashlight turns ON automatically.
4. Tap record. Tap again to stop. UI goes back to normal after.

🔦 Flashlight / Flash — What’s the difference?

  • Flash (⚡): Only flashes for a second when you take a photo. Modes: Off / On / Auto / Torch.
  • Flashlight / Torch (🔦): Stays ON the whole time while you record video or take photos. Great in the dark.
  • Tap ⚡ to change Flash mode. Tap 🔦 to turn Torch on/off.

🔄 How to switch Front / Back Camera

Tap the 🔄 button next to the shutter. Front = selfie (mirrored). Back = normal camera. On phones, back camera usually has flashlight.

🖼️ Gallery

Tap Gallery in bottom bar. Every photo/video you ever took in this app is there. Tap one to view, Share, Edit, or Download. It downloads with a name like:
LumenCam_2026-11-26_14-32-10_Dubai.jpg
If you enable Location in Settings, it adds your location.

✏️ Editor

1. Open Gallery → tap a photo → Edit.
2. Pick a filter, adjust Brightness / Contrast, Rotate, add text.
3. Tap Save Edited or Share.

↗️ Sharing

In Gallery or Editor, tap Share. On phone it opens WhatsApp / Instagram / Files. On computer it downloads or copies the file.

📍 Location in Filename

Settings → Save Location → ON → Tap Enable Location Now → Allow location. Next photos will include your city / coords in the filename.

⚙️ Light / Dark Mode

Settings → Appearance → Dark / Light. Space Video always uses Light Mode while recording, then goes back.

❓ Camera not working?

  • Use Chrome / Safari, not in-app browser.
  • Allow Camera + Microphone when asked.
  • Close other apps using camera (Zoom, WhatsApp).
  • Reload the page.
  • On iPhone: Settings → Safari → Camera → Allow.

❓ Flashlight not working?

Flashlight only works on Android Chrome with back camera, and only if your phone supports it. On iPhone, browsers block torch — use Flash (⚡) instead, it still flashes when you take the photo.
